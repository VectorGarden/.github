Single-purpose tools that do one thing and get out of the way. Most of them
exist because a card game needed one and nothing good was around. Everything
here is static, free, and runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, no
uploads, no telemetry.

## The mark

Three leaves rising from a seed, with a vector arrowhead cut out of the foliage
as negative space. The name came down from `reizu` → *rayzoo* → *ray zoo* →
*vector garden*, and the mark keeps both halves: the seed is the origin, the
arrowhead is the direction, the leaves are what grew.

| | Sprout | Light plum | Ink | Paper |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light surfaces | `#4C8B3A` | `#6B4080` | `#1C1C1A` | `#FAF8F2` |
| Dark surfaces | `#7DB85F` | `#A87DC4` | — | — |

The two colours sit too close to ink to survive a dark background unchanged, so
dark surfaces use the tinted pair instead.
